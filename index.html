About the book

An interactive fiction exploring the overlaps, echoes and analogies between the physicality of the book and the human body.

A story of transformation, sickness, death and, perhaps, rebirth.

By Carla Andrea del Moral Suarez,
Adrian Menendez de la Cuesta Gonzalez
and Cristina Jimenez Barranquero.

Originally published in 2017.
