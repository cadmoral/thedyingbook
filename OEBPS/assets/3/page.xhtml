Book, book... is it me or am I the text that you read and forget as you go by?

Do you miss going through the pages? Do you miss the smell?
Consider me as young or old. And for the smell I suggest your favorite rotting body odor.

Am I the same on paper than on screen?

Certainly, we are exposed to new conditions. They are already acting on us.

We endure them as we go through a never ending disease.

 

All in all, it is a pleasure to finally meet. Two individuals sharing the same fate.

Please let me feel you as you feel me. Touch my hand.

But maybe the disease is death itself and nothing is worse than death.

 

We are together on this trip.

 

 Without a starting point,

There is always an ending

that comes along.

 

 

 

 

 

Gute Reise to us all.
